## How to write a post

Create a new `.md` file in `content/blog/`. The file name becomes the URL slug, so use short, keyword-rich, hyphenated names.

## Tips for SEO and AEO

- **Lead with the answer.** Put a one- or two-sentence answer in `summary`. Answer engines love quotable snippets.
- **Use question-style headings** (`## What is …?`, `## How do I …?`) that match what people actually search.
- **Add FAQs** in frontmatter; they're rendered on the page and emitted as `FAQPage` structured data.
- **Keep `updated` current** when you revise a post, because freshness matters to both Google and AI assistants.
